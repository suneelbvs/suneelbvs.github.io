## The story in one line

**Find the hidden pocket → validate allostery → read the clinical PK failure correctly → preserve the pharmacophore while detuning metabolism.**

The case study emphasizes the human decisions at each gate rather than presenting computational chemistry or medicinal chemistry as an automated sequence of scores.
